## Temperature Effect on Diode (LTspice Simulation)

This simulation analyzes the behavior of a 1N4148 diode at two different temperatures: 25°C and 75°C using DC sweep analysis.

At 25°C, the diode shows a typical forward voltage of approximately 0.7V before significant current begins to flow. The reverse current is extremely small, indicating good blocking behavior.

At 75°C, the diode characteristics change noticeably. The forward voltage decreases, meaning the diode starts conducting at a lower voltage. Additionally, the reverse current increases significantly due to higher thermal energy generating more charge carriers inside the semiconductor.

Temperature affects diode behavior because semiconductor materials are highly sensitive to thermal energy. As temperature increases, more electrons gain energy and contribute to conduction. This reduces the barrier potential of the PN junction and increases leakage current.

This effect is important in real electronic systems because temperature variations can impact circuit performance, efficiency, and reliability. For example, excessive leakage current can lead to power loss, and reduced forward voltage can affect voltage regulation. Therefore, designers must consider temperature effects when selecting components and designing circuits, especially in high-temperature environments.
